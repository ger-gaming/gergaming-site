How can we help?

Email support@gergaming.com and we'll get back to you, usually within two business days. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if you're reporting a problem.

I bought Remove Ads or a skin, but it's not showing

Open the Skins Shop and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so they carry over to new devices and reinstalls.

What does Remove Ads do?

It removes banner ads and the ads shown between runs. You can still use Continue after a run ends, and with Remove Ads it no longer requires watching an ad.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are processed by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How does the Daily Challenge work?

Everyone in the world gets the same tower each day. It resets at midnight UTC (7:00 PM Eastern Standard Time). Play on consecutive days to build your streak.

What do the colored squares in my shared result mean?

Each square is one floor of your tower: 🟩 perfect, 🟨 good, 🟧 okay, 🟥 rough, and 💥 the block that ended your run.

My score isn't on the leaderboard

Leaderboards use Game Center. Check that you're signed in under Settings → Game Center on your iPhone, then play another run.

Will I lose my progress if I delete the app?

Your coins, unlocked skins earned with coins, best score and streak are stored on your device and are removed if you delete the app. Purchases made with real money can always be restored.
