Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

This policy explains how Drop Stack (the "App"), developed by Glenn Roberts ("we", "us"), handles information. We've kept it short and plain. If you have questions, email support@gergaming.com.

The short version

  • We don't ask you to create an account, and we don't collect your name, email or contacts.
  • Your game progress (coins, skins, scores, streak) stays on your device.
  • The App shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some device and usage information to serve and measure ads.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple, and leaderboards by Apple's Game Center.

1. Information stored on your device

The App saves your game data, such as coins, owned skins, settings, best scores, daily results and streak, locally on your device. We don't receive this data. It is deleted if you delete the App.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. To serve, limit, measure and prevent fraud in ads, Google may collect information such as:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), but only if you allow tracking when iOS asks
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • Device type, operating system, language and app version
  • Ad interactions, such as ads viewed or tapped
  • Diagnostic information, such as crashes related to ads

If you don't allow tracking, ads are still shown but are not personalized using your advertising identifier. Players in certain regions, such as the European Economic Area and the UK, are asked for consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can learn more about how Google uses information at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Buying Remove Ads turns off banner and between-run ads.

3. Purchases (Apple)

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or store your payment details. We receive only confirmation that a purchase was made, so we can unlock it in the App. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Leaderboards (Game Center)

If you're signed in to Game Center, the App submits your scores to Apple's Game Center leaderboards, where your Game Center nickname and scores may be visible to other players. This is managed by Apple under its privacy policy. You can manage Game Center in your iPhone's Settings.

5. How we use information

We use the services above to run the App, show ads, process purchases, display leaderboards and fix problems. We don't sell your personal information, and we don't use the data in this App to build profiles about you ourselves.

6. Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience game and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we'll help address it.

7. Your choices

  • Tracking: change your choice anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad consent (where applicable): you can change your choice by deleting and reinstalling the App, which shows the consent form again.
  • Game data: delete the App to remove all locally stored data.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, California, the EEA or the UK), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of personal information. Because we don't hold personal information ourselves, most requests are best directed to Google or Apple through the links above, but we're happy to help: email support@gergaming.com.

9. Data retention and security

We don't store personal information on our own servers. Information collected by Google and Apple is retained according to their policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
