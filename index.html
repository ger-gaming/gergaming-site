Drop Stack

A one-tap stacking game for iPhone

Tap. Drop. Stack.

Blocks slide back and forth. Tap to drop each one onto the tower. Line it up perfectly to keep it whole; miss, and the overhang gets sliced off. How high can you go?

  • Daily Challenge: the same tower for everyone each day. Share your result and keep your streak alive.
  • Endless: climb as high as you can and chase your best score.
  • Leaderboards: compare scores with friends and players worldwide through Game Center.
  • Skins: unlock new looks for your tower, and meet Cubby, who rides along on top.
